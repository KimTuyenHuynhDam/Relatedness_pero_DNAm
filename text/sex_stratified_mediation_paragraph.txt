Sex-stratified mediation analyses revealed distinct patterns. In F (n = 47), PC1 showed an indirect-only effect (ACME = -0.00177, 95% CI [-0.00323, -0.00045], p = 0.01), while the direct (ADE = 0.00079, p = 0.404) and total effects based on the same model (Total = -0.00098, p = 0.261) were not significant; PC2 showed no significant mediation (ACME p = 0.263). In M (n = 49), PC1 exhibited a small direct effect (ADE = -0.00243, 95% CI [-0.00315, -0.00024], p = 0.0352), with negligible mediation (ACME p = 0.442); by contrast, PC2 mediated the association (ACME = -0.00063, 95% CI [-0.00143, -0.00011], p = 0.0328). Benjamini–Hochberg q-values (by sex) appear in figure labels.
